Swedish meatballs

Delicious swedish meatballs that go perfectly with mashed potatoes, fries or boiled carrots!

This recipe will teach you how to create a timeless swedish classic enjoyed in all IKEA's around the world.

Ingredients:

  • 2 dl of whipping cream
  • 1 dl of breadcrumbs
  • 1 large onion
  • 800g of beef minced meat
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp of salt
  • 1/2 tsp of black pepper
  • 1/3 tsp of allspice
  • 1/4 tsp of white pepper

Steps:

  1. Mix the cream and breadcrumbs together.
  2. Finely chop the onion, fry it in Oivarine fat in a pan until soft.
  3. Combine all the ingredients. Mix the meatball dough until smooth.
  4. Shape the dough into large meatballs with water-moistened hands.
  5. Bake the meatballs on two baking trays on a baking sheet in the middle/top of the oven at 250 degrees celsius for about 15 minutes or until cooked.
